# Sushi logo: usage guide

**Idea:** a maki piece standing up, with a pair of eyes on the nori. The pet that watches your agents.

## Files

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `symbol.svg` | Main symbol, in colour, for sizes of 32 px and up |
| `symbol-small.svg` | Simplified cut (bigger eyes), for 16-31 px: favicon, tab, tray |
| `horizontal.svg` | Symbol + wordmark, on light backgrounds |
| `horizontal-reversed.svg`, `symbol-reversed.svg` | On dark backgrounds (cream outline) |
| `symbol-black.svg`, `horizontal-black.svg` | One colour, for print, stamps, embossing, fax |
| `app-icon.svg`, `maskable.svg`, `icon-*.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png` | App and web icons |
| `favicon.ico`, `favicon.svg`, `favicon-*.png`, `head-snippet.html`, `site.webmanifest` | Favicon set |
| `board.html` | Presentation board with mockups |

## Colours

| Role | HEX | RGB |
|---|---|---|
| Salmon (filling) | `#FF7A59` | 255, 122, 89 |
| Ink (outline, wordmark) | `#1B1B1F` | 27, 27, 31 |
| Nori | `#26262B` | 38, 38, 43 |
| Rice | `#FBF6EC` | 251, 246, 236 |

CMYK and Pantone values are not defined yet: match them on a printed proof before ordering anything.
The wordmark is always ink `#1B1B1F` on light, white on dark.

## Clear space and size

- Clear space: at least **1/4 of the symbol height** on every side.
- Minimum size: symbol **16 px** (use `symbol-small.svg` under 32 px); horizontal lockup **96 px** wide; in print, symbol 8 mm.

## Do

- Use the symbol on light or dark neutral backgrounds, or on the cream tile for app icons.
- Use the one-colour black version when colour isn't available.

## Don't

- Don't stretch, rotate or recolour the salmon, nori or rice.
- Don't remove the eyes, and don't add a mouth, shadows or gradients.
- Don't use the colour symbol on busy photos or on coral backgrounds.
- Don't retype the wordmark: use the supplied outlines.

## Notes

- The wordmark is Inter Medium converted to outlines. Inter is under the SIL OFL licence, which allows logo use.
- No trademark search has been done. Before commercial use, search trademark databases and do a reverse image search.
- The mono version loses the nori/rice/salmon distinction by design: the ring and the eyes keep it readable.
